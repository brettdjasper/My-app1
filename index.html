The Team

Alex Waterbury

Managing Partner

Alex formed WDP after 26 years as a leader in real estate advisory, asset management, and ground-up development. He has worked for firms large and small, public and private – from local entrepreneurs to a global private equity firm – and invested in communities across the country. Throughout, housing development has been his core passion and expertise, ranging from single-family land planning to high-density urban infill construction. His most recent experience building an affordable housing platform has prepared him to address one of the nation’s most pressing challenges. With a focus on supply-constrained markets in the Western US, and embracing difficult sites that require creativity and ample patience, he has founded WDP to be a true solutions-oriented development partner.

Natalie W. Miles

Design, Operations, Accounting

Natalie brings a wealth of experiences in branding and graphic design to the WDP platform, with a degree from the Parsons School of Design and roles at HDR Architects, Alfatech, and Wells Fargo. More recently, she has honed a broader set of real estate property management and operations skills, with proficiency in Yardi, Quickbooks, and Adobe Suite.
